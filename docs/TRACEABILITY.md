# Requirement traceability

Filled in by Claude Code at the end of each phase. One row per requirement ID from Appendix A of `docs/FDS.md`. The phase "supplements" is the change after phase 8 that brought the activity types and rates in line with the BMC timesheet. The phase "same-day" is the change of FDS 4.4 that lets an article 17 worker declare several activities on one day. The phase "security" is the change of FDS 4.5 that followed a security audit of the application. The phase "report calendar" is the change of FDS 7.1 that shows the accepted activities of the month as a calendar on the dashboard, and of FDS 7.5 that moves the monthly report between months with arrows. The phase "activities tab" is the change of FDS 7 and 7.8 that adds the Activities tab and reorders the admin tabs. The phase "rc1 review" is the fix, found in the review before release 3.0.0rc1, of worker actions that could run on values the worker did not choose: a click on an expired sign-in ran after the next sign-in, and a date or a choice that changed between the page and the click was replaced. The phase "rc1" is the change of FDS 4.8 that moves the version from betas to release candidates. The phase "android" is the change of FDS 4.10 that serves a web app manifest for the Android home screen.
